Factions and territory

Creator-established factions and territory. Claims preserve inhabitants and resources. Separately enabled border rules can block hostile travel, trade and knowledge. Neutral, allied and unclaimed borders stay open. Combat requires explicitly enabled conflict rules. Ask the advisor to rename, recolor or remove an unclaimed faction.

Garrison service

Local residents remain in population and eat normal settlement meals. Healthy members serve instead of farming or researching; reserved slots stay home. Service pauses below the food threshold or on flooded/submerged land. Combat requires explicitly enabled conflict rules.

Move garrison troops

Send healthy garrison members with real provisions through own or allied land. Arrival reinforces an own-faction settlement. This does not start combat.

Departure reduces the home garrison target. Provision the planned trip and leave food reserves for remaining residents. Ask the advisor to redirect, provision or dock a waiting army.

Conflict rules

Hostile neighbors can launch supplied expeditions automatically. Civilian closures do not stop invasions. Healthy combatants can die; capture preserves civilians, food and buildings. Surviving attackers return home. Pausing cancels pending attacks.

Research and technology

Settlements choose projects automatically. Healthy residents research instead of farming, protecting food reserves. Improvements begin the day after completion. Filtration improves installed sanitation plants; it does not build them. Ask the advisor to define technologies, tune research rates or assign a specific project. Separately enabled knowledge exchange accelerates local research through communicating neighboring settlements.

Health and recovery

Ask the advisor to change recovery and immunity-loss rates. People carry their health state when moving. Contact spread is separately activated; travel closures block visits, not local mixing.

Settlement and food

Place inhabitants explicitly. Farming needs suitable temperature and water. Review five-day effects before Apply.

Ask the advisor to adjust capacity, farming, population or demographic rules, or remove a settlement.

Everyday neighbor travel

People collect surplus food, work on neighboring farms, or explore open land. Trips return the same day. Travel closures block both directions; food collection also respects food-sharing permissions. Ask the advisor to adjust daily rates and reserves.

Automatic relocation

People move toward better food reserves and housing. Departures need no approval; timed journeys carry inhabitants and food. Ask the advisor to tune rates and thresholds.

Journeys and migration

Move inhabitants between existing settlements along the shortest open land path. Food is carried from this settlement. Ask the advisor to carry other stocks, provision, redirect or unload a journey.

Resource routes and travel

Named routes move custom stocks to adjacent land zones. Create a stock through the advisor first. Food sharing has its own permissions.

Ask the advisor to revise, pause or remove existing routes.
